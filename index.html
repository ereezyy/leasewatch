FAQ

Which beat stores work with LeaseWatch?

Any store where you can export sales or customer CSVs (starting with BeatStars) plus manual entry for Airbit, Shopify, or direct PayPal/Stripe sales.

Does LeaseWatch send emails or copyright strikes automatically?

No. LeaseWatch never contacts your buyers or files takedowns. It flags cap milestones and prepares an upgrade message and payment link for you to send when you choose.

How are songs matched if the artist didn't credit me?

You can include a one-click claim link in your delivery email, and LeaseWatch also suggests candidate matches based on buyer name, beat title, and audio/release metadata for you to confirm.

What will it cost?

Planned pricing starts at $0/month for up to 25 tracked leases, and $15/month for up to 500 leases with daily checks and Stripe upgrade links.
